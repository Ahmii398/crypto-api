# GSMS research report

Generated by `python -m gsms.report` from the files in `results/`. R = the amount risked on one trade; with 1 % of the account at risk, 1 R = 1 % of the account.

## 1. Bottom line

- **No configuration of this system has a statistically validated edge on gold or silver.** 5,061 parameter sets were tested on each of 14 symbol/timeframe datasets (70,854 backtests) with realistic costs.
- The six presets were chosen on 2010-2021 data only. On the untouched 2022-2026 data **5 of 6 lost money** (Gold 30m Conservative, Gold 2h Balanced, Gold 1h Aggressive, Silver 2h Conservative, Silver 30m Aggressive); only Silver 2h Balanced was profitable, and its 95 % confidence interval still includes zero (section 8).
- Gold (XAUUSD): of all 92 two-sided supply/demand strategies that were profitable in both 2010-2017 and 2018-2021, 39% were profitable in 2022-2026; median result -0.040 R per trade after costs, +0.024 R before costs.
- Silver (XAGUSD): of all 17 two-sided supply/demand strategies that were profitable in both 2010-2017 and 2018-2021, 24% were profitable in 2022-2026; median result -0.128 R per trade after costs, -0.021 R before costs.
- Walk-forward (re-optimise on 4 years, trade the next year, 2014-2026) lost money on all 5 datasets it was run on (section 8).
- Before costs the typical parameter set earns about 0 R per trade (median -0.020 to +0.017 R across datasets), and how a parameter set did before costs in 2010-2017 says almost nothing about 2018-2021: the rank correlation is -0.05 to +0.06 on 30m-4h. Costs then decide the result: the median cost per trade runs from 0.05 R (gold, slowest timeframe) to 0.72 R (silver, fastest), and is about 2.5 times larger on silver than on gold on the same timeframe (section 3).
- What is solid: the indicator and the strategy do what the rules say. The Pine scripts match the Python engine trade for trade in PineTS; TradingView results are not recorded yet (section 10).
- Practical reading: use the indicator as a chart-reading aid (zones, sweeps, patterns, alerts). Do not expect the presets to be profitable; the evidence says they are not.
